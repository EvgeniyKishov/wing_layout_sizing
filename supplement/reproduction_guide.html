Supplementary Reproducibility guide

Purpose and access

This guide accompanies the revision40 supplementary publication update, 19 November 2026. Paths are relative to repository root unless stated otherwise. Use the Git commit containing this updated guide and supplementary_materials.pdf as the fixed reference for this publication. The preceding supplementary evidence snapshot remains available at commit 1d5833960ae3f2c56e94312c881fe2b86efe24a5 at https://github.com/EvgeniyKishov/wing_layout_sizing. No scientific calculation was run for this publication update.

Consolidated Supplementary Materials

The separate supplement/supplementary_materials.pdf collects Figures S1-S12 and Tables S1-S4 in one 15-page document: panel-fit geometry (S1-S2), filter-radius sensitivity (Tables S1-S2 and Figures S3-S8), four-layout comparison (Tables S3-S4 and Figures S9-S10), and reinforcement patterns (S11-S12). It preserves the saved figures, table values and manuscript numbering. This reproduction guide remains a separate PDF. Individual study PDFs, machine-readable evidence and source records remain in their original repository locations. The consolidated PDF was added after commit 1d58339; its internal citation to that earlier commit identifies the source evidence snapshot, not the location of the consolidated PDF itself.

Three levels of reproduction

Inspection: open the JSON summaries, reports and supplementary PDFs; NPZ arrays require NumPy. Plot regeneration: the package-root plot_reference_results.py reads the four bundled fine histories without ANSYS or MATLAB. Other copied drawing/audit scripts retain original-project paths and dependencies and are reference source, not verified portable entry points. Solver reruns: consult package README.md and METHOD.md, use fresh output directories and licensed ANSYS/MATLAB installations. Match the recorded settings, load source and study-specific update law. Software versions remain available from the authors; dependencies are unpinned, so cross-installation identity is not guaranteed.

Problem and acceptance scope

Equation (1), Section 2.1 and Figures 1-2 define the objective, variables and workflow. Wing feasibility means filtered face stress <=300 MPa and lowest positive buckling factor >=1 on the assessment mesh under one prescribed aerodynamic load. Restricted screening plus heuristic sizing does not establish joint or global optimality. The two-rib design is the lightest evaluated fine candidate; 16-rib validation is reference-specific. Local strength, omitted sandwich failure modes and manufacturing readiness are not established.

Aerodynamic inputs and loading views

Read evidence/aerodynamic_input_provenance/provenance.json, input_manifest.json, saved_bridge_run.m and reproduction_comparison.json in that directory. These identify saved inputs, invocation, defaults and the existing byte-identical export check. evidence/loading_view_redraw/aerodynamic_loading_data.npz and redraw_audit.json document Figure 3. Package-only examples/fokker/ contains the structural example inputs and prescribed force field. Prescribed forces stay fixed during sizing; mesh/layout changes remap the same source forces conservatively. Byte identity is computational reproduction, not physical validation.

Plate and T-joint checks

supplement/plate_benchmark_draft.pdf and plate_comparison_data.json describe the plate benchmark; package-only reference_results/plate/ retains stage exports. The plate has its own update settings and is not identical to wing sizing. evidence/tjoint_clarification/RESULTS.txt and comparison.json retain the completed linear-static T-joint study; run_tjoint_filter_check.py is its source reference. The original completed run is runs/tjoint_filter_v3_20261014 in author storage. Earlier trial runs are not the reported evidence.

Four-layout sizing and ranking

Read supplement/four_layouts/four_layouts_supplement.pdf (Tables S3-S4, Figures S9-S10) and evidence/four_layouts/comparison.json and verification.json. Package-only reference_results/fine/ribs_02, ribs_08, ribs_16 and ribs_25 each contain mesh.npz, history.json, summary.json, fsd_fields.npz and buckling_fields.npz. reference_results/fine_comparison.json includes all four cases. Only final FSD and reinforced field snapshots are bundled; all solved states are recorded in histories, while intermediate arrays and native checkpoints remain in author storage. The two-rib original is runs/fokker_fine_2ribs_20261119/evaluations/ribs_02.

Separate relaxed-FSD mesh check

Read supplement/fine_fsd_mesh_check/STUDY_SUMMARY.txt and ACCEPTED_STUDY.json. These unchanged saved records describe the 20-rib relaxed-FSD setup, stopping criteria and two separate frozen-thickness refinement pairs. For Design B, iteration037, refinement from 48,728 to 194,912 elements preserves mass 309.417902 kg; filtered peaks are 297.757045 and 326.306213 MPa, and raw peaks are 489.778526 and 673.012819 MPa. The 9.588075% filtered change passes the 10% tolerance adopted after reviewing results, while the refined 300 MPa constraint fails. Buckling was not verified. Design A uses a different thickness field; the pairs are not a single three-mesh convergence sequence. The summary records the relaxed update, 0.30 m filter and original run locations. Native fields remain in author storage. These records were added in manuscript review36 and are included in this repository update. They are absent from the preceding GitHub commit 319021c.

Radius and reinforcement studies

supplement/radius_sensitivity/radius_sensitivity_supplement.pdf (Tables S1-S2, Figures S3-S8) and evidence/radius_sensitivity/comparison.json, baseline_audit.json and verification.json document complete sizing at 0.15, 0.30 and 0.60 m, including reuse of the 0.30 m baseline. supplement/reinforcement_patterns/ contains Figures S11-S12 and the saved-field audit; its REPRODUCTION_NOTE.txt identifies original-project dependencies. evidence/reinforcement_expression_sources/SOURCE_RECORD.json identifies the update implementations. These assessments concern the 16-rib reference.

Rib endpoint and section geometry

Read evidence/submodel_baseline_20261117_comparison.json, distributed_load_sweep_20261118_comparison.json, rib_spatial_quality_audit_20261118_audit.json and rib_targeted_refinement_20261118_comparison.json. They document original-mesh reproduction, distributed-force checks, refinement and fixed-distance sampling. Scripts alongside them require original project data, including inherited cut motion. Original run roots: runs/skin_front_spar_submodel_baseline_20261117, runs/submodel_distributed_load_sweep_20261118 and runs/rib_endpoint_targeted_refinement_20261118. supplement/panel_fit_audit/ contains saved section/offset evidence for Figures S1-S2. Peak growth and interior stabilization support a qualified singularity interpretation, not a local strength margin.

Shell-solid comparison

evidence/shell_solid_validation/ contains preparation.json, integration.json, boundary_audit.json, boundary_release.json, three_mesh.json and finest_qa.json. Read its README.txt for original-run provenance. Only three completed released-boundary solid meshes contribute to the reported sequence; a further stopped refinement supplies no usable result. Interior stress agreement does not establish converged cut reactions or joint/failure validation. Native fields and complete boundary data are not all bundled.

Storage and deposition

The package includes compact reference results, revision evidence, supplements and a SHA256 manifest. It excludes native solver binaries, all intermediate arrays and parts of the original project tree. Native files and additional reproduction details can be requested from the corresponding author (chertykovceva.vo@ssau.ru). Verified compressed archives are retained in author storage; paths inside saved provenance records describe that storage, not necessarily files shipped in this bundle. No automatic full rerun or bitwise reproducibility claim is made. The project URL is https://github.com/EvgeniyKishov/wing_layout_sizing; select the commit containing this revision40 guide for the consolidated supplementary PDF and current guide; commits 1d58339 and 319021c retain earlier snapshots.
