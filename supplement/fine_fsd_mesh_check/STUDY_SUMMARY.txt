ACCEPTED CONCLUSION
The 0.30 m area-weighted conical scalar stress filter reduces mesh sensitivity
of the monitored peak in the investigated models and supports practical FSD
sizing. For the fine-optimized design, one frozen-design refinement meets the
user-adopted 10% tolerance. This tolerance was adopted after reviewing results;
it is an engineering acceptance choice, not a predeclared validation standard.

FSD RULES AND STOPPING
Start: uniform total thickness 10 mm. Each fine element has an independent
thickness variable. Update in SI: clip(0.5*t*(1+sigma_filtered/285e6),
0.000625,0.15). Face/core/face proportions 0.4/0.2/0.4.
Active: thickness strictly inside physical bounds AND filtered stress outside
inclusive 285..315 MPa. Denominator: all thickness variables.
Stop: peak filtered stress <=300 MPa AND (active fraction <5% OR proposed
maximum relative thickness change <=1%). Fine run stopped at iteration37
(38 solved states): 2413/48728 active =4.951978%; proposed change4.318305%.
It stopped by active fraction, not by the 1% thickness-change criterion.

FILTER AND SOLVER
Area-weighted conical kernel max(0,1-d/R), R=0.30 m, row normalized, actual3D
centroid distances. Filter separately over upper/lower skin, front/rear spar,
and each20 ribs (24 groups). Input: per-element maximum of element-averaged
von Mises stress at BOT/MID/TOP of both metal face layers. Raw stresses retained.
This regularizes the scalar sizing measure; it does not modify the FE stiffness,
equilibrium solution, stress tensor, or geometric stiffness/buckling prestress.
SHELL181 full integration; ANSYS SMP four cores. Four third-mesh children
inherit every fine section. Connectivity, support nodes, load resultants,
native element areas and mass conservation checked. All39 new analyses had
zero solver errors. Existing15 FSD/filter tests passed.

LIMITS TO KEEP IN THE DRAFT
- The26.60% and9.59% jumps belong to DIFFERENT optimized thickness fields;
  do not connect them as a three-point convergence curve for one fixed design.
- A single accepted refinement pair does not establish asymptotic convergence
  or guarantee that further refinement will produce smaller changes.
- Raw peak stresses remain high; averaging is not proof of local strength.
- No buckling solve/correction for this fine FSD design was performed here.
  Do not claim buckling convergence or full structural feasibility.
- All20 ribs have0.625 mm total thickness; front spar0.625..4.930857 mm,
  rear spar0.625..0.821989 mm. These are FSD-stage results before reinforcement.
